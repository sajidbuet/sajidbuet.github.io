Music has always been a personal creative outlet alongside my academic and research activities. I am a hobby musician, primarily a self-taught guitarist, and I enjoy exploring acoustic interpretations, simple arrangements, and experimental compositions. Music, for me, complements engineering: it sharpens intuition, pattern recognition, and creative synthesis.

Over time, I have also developed hands-on familiarity with digital music production tools such as Logic Pro and GarageBand, using them for recording, mixing, and basic sound design. While not a professional pursuit, music provides a valuable space for reflection, creativity, and storytelling.

Suno AI is one of the most fascinating tools that I find great joy in using lately, as it lets me experiment with new styles.
